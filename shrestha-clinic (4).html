About the Clinic

Expert Care Under One Roof

Shrestha Hearing, Vertigo, Speech Care & Research Centre is Bhubaneswar's premier audiological and vestibular clinic, located at Acharya Vihar on Sachivalaya Marg. We combine advanced diagnostic technology with compassionate, patient-centred care.

Patients travel from across Odisha and neighbouring states like Andhra Pradesh to receive treatment here — particularly for complex vertigo and hearing conditions that have gone unresolved elsewhere.

State-of-the-art VNG, ENG, VEMP, and PTA diagnostic equipment
Clean, modern, and comfortable clinic environment
Personalised treatment plans with expert follow-up care
Same-day vertigo treatment with proven results for BPPV patients
